## A4. Transport
- U-Pass in fees: Fall 2026 grad schedule https://www.uregina.ca/financial-services/assets/pdf/fee-schedules/202630-grad-studies-tuition-schedule-may-15.pdf: "students enrolled in at least one on-campus course ... will be assessed an additional $90.00 U-Pass fee"; "U-Pass and the $90.00 fee are mandatory". FAQ https://www.uregina.ca/students/services-faq.html: "The cost is $90 per semester." Opt-out allowed if you "Live in University of Regina residence" or within 1 km of campus. => counted as a mandatory fee (tuition side); unlimited Regina Transit.
- City of Regina fares https://www.regina.ca/transportation-roads-parking/transit/fares-passes/ (post 8 Apr 2026 increase): Adult (19-64) Monthly "$97", Semi-Annual "$488"; Post Secondary Monthly "$86", Four Month "$286"; single ride stored value "$3.20", cash "$3.50"; monthly cap "$97". Increase notice https://www.regina.ca/news/Transit-Fares-Increasing/ (31 Mar 2026): "Effective Wednesday, April 8, Transit fares in Regina will increase".
- U-Pass covers Spring/Summer too if registered in an on-campus course (fee "once-per-semester"); if not registered in summer, would need city pass (paraphrase).

## A6. Health
- Health & Dental plan in grad fees (Fall 2026 schedule): "assessed an additional $225.85 Health & Dental fee"; "only assessed in the fall semester for health and dental coverage for 12 months beginning September 1"; mandatory unless proof of comparable coverage. (Already a tuition-side fee.)
- Saskatchewan Health Card: https://www.uregina.ca/ur-international/student-services/health-card.html — "a free and valuable personal identification card"; "International students who hold a valid study permit are eligible". eHealth Saskatchewan https://www.ehealthsask.ca/residents/health-cards/apply-for-a-health-card/eligibility-for-health-benefits lists "an international student" living in Saskatchewan among eligible. No premium. Gap coverage before card arrives: UNCONFIRMED cost (search snippet says coverage begins on receipt of card — not verified).

## A. University of Guelph

### A1. Residence: eligibility, guarantee, dates
- Apply page https://www.uoguelph.ca/housing/apply , section "Graduate Students": "If you are a new international graduate student entering a master's program at the University of Guelph, you are guaranteed University housing for the duration of your studies." Conditions: "Submit a Residence Application by June 1;" "Pay the $750 residence deposit by June 1; and" "Accept their offer of admission to the University of Guelph by June 1." Canadian grads: "residence spaces are not guaranteed."

- Important dates https://www.uoguelph.ca/housing/important-dates : "Monday, February 1, 2027 Fall 2027 - Winter 2028 Residence Applications OPEN"; "Tuesday, June 1, 2027 ... Residence Application & Deposit Deadline for: ... New Graduate students". 2026-27 pattern: residences open Sept 4-5, close Dec 22 (Fall), reopen Jan 9, close "Saturday, May 1, 2027" (Winter).
- Contract https://www.uoguelph.ca/housing/residence-contract : "East Village Townhouses, University Houses and West Residence, which are typically reserved for Returning Resident Applicants or mature, graduate or transfer students." Summer: "Only the East Village Townhouses, University Houses, and West Residences operate during the summer semester." "No Deposit is required for a Summer Contract." "Meal plans are not offered during the Summer Semester." Mandatory Marsh tenant insurance "included in the Residence fees". Check-out exceptions "normally apply for DVM or graduate students only."
- Communities https://www.uoguelph.ca/housing/communities : "Over 95% of our residence spaces are reserved for first year students".
- Fall/Winter contract = 2 semesters (~Sept to end April, ~8 months); holiday break NOT covered (see A2). Summer = separate contract.

### Verdict
- Cheapest realistic: Brandon shared off-campus ~18,100/yr; Brandon on-campus ~19,800-20,300 (all meals included, but no kitchens and summer stay only if studying). Guelph: on-campus self-catering single ~22,200 and off-campus shared ~22,500 are nearly equal; on-campus wins on certainty (guarantee, furnished, utilities/internet included, no lease).

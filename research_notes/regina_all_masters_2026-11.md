# University of Regina: all master's programs, re-checked live (Nov 2026)

Source pages (headless Chromium, read in full):
- Master's Programs list: https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/programs/masters-programs.html
- Engineering calendar pages: https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-{ese,erse,evse,ise,pse,pseng,sse}.html

## Findings
- No Biomedical Engineering master's exists at Regina. Engineering master's listed: Electronic Systems (MASc/MEng), Energy Systems (ERSE), Environmental Systems (MASc/MEng), Industrial Systems (MASc/MEng), Petroleum Systems (MASc/MEng), Process Systems (MASc/MEng), Software Systems (MASc/MEng). CORRECTION: the "Suspended for one year, effective 202630" note on the list belongs to the embedded MCert in Artificial Intelligence in Engineering, not to SSE itself (per the SSE calendar page; see regina_masters_table_A.md).
- M.Eng. route totals (calendar tables): ESE project 30 ch, co-op 30; ISE course 39 ch, project 30, co-op 30; EVSE project 30, co-op 30; PSE project 30, co-op 30; PSEng project 30, co-op 30; ERSE project 30, co-op 30.
- ISE "course" route is coursework only (12 x 3-ch courses + ENGG 701/702/703), 39 ch. At the 2026-27 M.Eng rate ($597.75/ch + $1,487.25 surcharge/term) it exceeds CAD 30,000 even in 4 terms (about $31,000) — FAIL on cost.
- Project routes "attract practicing engineers" (calendar wording, all streams). Supervisor: ESE/SSE "normally be assigned a project supervisor"; Engineering page: "Electronics MENG students do not need to contact professors"; for other streams contacting a professor is recommended, not required ("You may still apply if you have not made contact").
- ISE research areas: "manufacturing, control, process engineering and energy systems"; embedded certificates in Advanced Manufacturing and Engineering Management. ESE areas: "RF communication, machine learning, smart grid, ... signal/image processing, FPGA system design, neural computing".
- Science MSc programs (Biology, Chemistry/Biochemistry, Computer Science, Earth Sciences, Mathematics/Statistics, Physics) and KHS MSc are thesis programs needing a supervisor. JSGS (MHA, MPA, MPP) and Levene (MBA, EMBA, MAdmin Leadership, MHRM, MSc Organization Studies) were checked earlier (cost / bar).
- All programs share the FGSR minimum: "A graduating average of 70 per cent" (75% for English, History, Math/Stats, MPA).

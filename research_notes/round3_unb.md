## 1. SGS general rules (opened in full)

Source A: SGS General Regulations, "4. Admissions" — https://www.unb.ca/gradstudies/current/resources/regulations-and-guidelines/regulations/admissions.html
- General minimum: "a bachelor's degree ... with a cumulative grade point average of at least 3.0."
- Foreign applicants: "at least a B average (North American System) or upper second class standing (British System)". "the requirement for specific graduate programs may be higher."
- Years counted: "cumulative" (paraphrase: whole degree; no last-2-years / last-60-credits rule stated at SGS level).
- No percentage, no country table, no Lebanon row, no repeated-course rule on this page.
- "Admissions decisions are not appealable." "Admission ... is limited and competitive".
- Qualifying period: for inadequate background "where the standard of academic performance otherwise meets the stipulated admission standards" (so NOT a route for low grades).
- Probationary Period (Student on Probation): when undergrad performance "does not meet the canonical admission standards of the SGS, but where there are other very strong indications of potential." Example given: "cumulative GPA below 3.0 (B)" with "an acceptable explanation" AND "evidence also of exceptional (A range) performance in the last two years of study." "granted only on the basis of very strong supporting evidence". Must get "a grade of B or better in the first four courses"; assessment on "15 ch of graduate level courses in course based programs". (Paraphrase: route exists but needs A-range last two years.)

Source B: SGS FAQ — https://www.unb.ca/gradstudies/admissions/faq.html
- WES: "Can I submit a WES evaluation instead of transcripts?" -> "Yes, a course by course (CxC) WES evaluation is acceptable." (accepted as alternative, not required)
- Deadline: global SGS deadline is "the add/drop date for full session courses"; departments may set earlier deadlines.
- Decisions: "it can take up to six months".

Source C: SGS International Applicants — https://www.unb.ca/gradstudies/admissions/international.html (section "Language proficiency test scores")
- Duolingo: "Minimum overall score of 125." "Minimum subscore of 105."
- IELTS: "Academic Module" "Minimum overall band score of 7." "One Skill Retake accepted." "Online version not accepted." (General Training not listed -> not accepted at SGS level.)
- "The School of Graduate Studies will not issue waivers based on language tests."
- "Some programs require a higher standard on these tests".
- Health: "all international students who have student visas must purchase international health insurance" (Guard.Me, included in fees).
- PAL: "Effective Jan. 1, 2026, international graduate students ... exempt from requiring a Provincial Attestation Letter".
- Application fee (SGS admissions index https://www.unb.ca/gradstudies/admissions/index.html): "International - $125.00".

## 4. Cost — course-based Master of Engineering, 2026-27 (applies to ME, GGE, ECE, Civil MEng)
Source: "UNB Fredericton 2026-2027 Tuition & Fees (effective September 1, 2026) Master of Engineering - Course Based - Full Time" — https://www.unb.ca/finance/_assets/documents/financial-services/fredericton-tuition/grad/course-based/fred_gr_cb_eng_ft.pdf (opened in full). Index page: https://www.unb.ca/finance/financial-services/graduate/fredericton.html
International: "Tuition (per course) 1,909.00"; "Mandatory Fees1 (per term) 107.25" ("Applies to only two terms per academic year"); "Mandatory Fees2 (per course) 41.00"; GSA "74.00" per term; "GSA Health and Dental 730.00" (Fall; 590.00 for January start; opt-out process referenced); "GSA Universal Bus Pass 176.00" (Fall; 90.00 Jan start); "International Emergency Health Ins. 200.00" per term; "International Health Travel Insurance 64.50" once per 12 months. "A minimum of 3 courses is required per term for full time status." Online course fee 150/course "may apply".
Health page https://www.unb.ca/finance/financial-services/health-insurance.html : guard.me "mandatory"; "$200 per person for a four-month term"; opt-out only with "a valid New Brunswick Medicare card" (or SNB letter); Annual Excursion "$64.50/year" mandatory.

Arithmetic (10 courses x (1,909 + 41) = 19,500):
- A. 3 terms (F/W/S, 12 months; GGE says 3-4 terms): 19,500 + 2x107.25 (214.50) + 3x74 (222) + 730 + 176 + 3x200 (600) + 64.50 = 21,507.00
- B. 4 terms (F/W/S/F): A + 107.25 + 74 + 730 + 176 + 200 + 64.50 = 22,858.75
- C. 5 terms over 24 months (F/W/S/F/W, ME "normally ... approximately 24 months"): 19,500 + 4x107.25 (429) + 5x74 (370) + 2x730 (1,460) + 2x176 (352) + 5x200 (1,000) + 2x64.50 (129) = 23,240.00
- D. 6 terms (24 months incl. 2nd summer): C + 74 + 200 = 23,514.00
- E. Risk: ME may require extra courses for non-ME degree. 12 courses, 6 terms: D + 2x1,950 = 27,414.00 -> FEE-INCREASE RISK flag (>= 27,000). 11 courses, 6 terms: 25,464.00.
- Fall 2027 entry will be billed on 2027-28 rates (not yet published); +5% on C = ~24,400.
- Excludes CAD 125 application fee. Emergency health 200/term can drop once NB Medicare obtained (lowers cost).
All A-D PASS F3 (< 27,000).

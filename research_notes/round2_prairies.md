### F1 Admission bar — PASS (thin)
- Eligibility page, "Master's Degree": "A graduating average of at least 70 per cent from a four-year baccalaureate degree (or equivalent)" (75% only for English, History, Math & Stats, MPA).
- Eligibility page, "International Students": "The equivalent score to 70% at the U of R is 2.67 out of 4.00 as per the WES calculator". The same paragraph deliberately links to the University of Guelph credential list as a source ("The University of Guelph maintains a list of credentials earned in other countries") — it is NOT a copy-paste error as the prior round assumed; it is an external reference. No Regina-owned Lebanon row exists.
- Basis = full four-year graduating average. Fallback: "marginally lower four-year average may be accepted if ... last two complete years is significantly higher (75 per cent or more)" (applicant's last 2 yrs ~2.90 ≈ 72-73% → fallback does not help).
- Calendar p.6 "Classification of Students": Fully-qualified = "4-year degree with a minimum grade point average of 70% ... in a field closely related". No honours requirement ("honours baccalaureate or a 4-year degree").
- WES mandatory for Engineering: Eligibility page "Engineering and Applied Science — Students with international credentials MUST provide all post-secondary transcripts ... through the WES ICAP course by course evaluation." Exemption effective 202710 covers only MASc/PhD ESE and (202730) MASc/PhD ISE — not M.Eng. Engineering page: "MUST submit post-secondary transcripts and degree certificates through WES."
- Repeated courses: no Regina rule for foreign transcripts found; since WES ICAP is mandatory, WES's own CGPA (2.68, F* attempts excluded) is the number admissions sees. Cuts in applicant's favour. Margin 0.01. Plus "All graduate programs ... are competitive" (eligibility page) — minimum does not guarantee admission.
- Qualifying risk: ESE calendar: applicants lacking "Engineering design, law, professionalism and ethics, and economics may be admitted as 'qualifying students.'" Qualifying courses "may not be credited towards graduate degree programs" (calendar p.6) → possible extra cost/time.

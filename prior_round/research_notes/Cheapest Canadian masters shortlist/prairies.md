## Q1. Which Prairie programs survive all six filters? (Overall verdicts)

### Takeaway
Only one family of programs survives: the **University of Regina M.Eng. (project/course-based, 30 credit hours)**, in Software Systems, Industrial Systems, Electronic Systems, Environmental Systems, Process Systems or Petroleum Systems. It is a provisional PASS. The admission bar is 70%, and Regina's FGSR says that equals **2.67/4.00 on WES**. The applicant's WES cumulative GPA is 2.68, so they clear it by only 0.01. The project supervisor is assigned by the program, and total cost is probably about CAD 21k–29k. The exact international fee is UNCONFIRMED and the high end is a fee-increase risk. Everything else checked fails:
- **Manitoba, Brandon and Lethbridge:** the bar is a plain B, 3.0, which fails Filter 1.
- **USask:** its Lebanon conversion row is "15 out of 20", above the applicant's 14.1/20.
- **Winnipeg:** its Lebanon row is also 15/20, and it applies to all programs.
- **Regina MHA, MPA and Levene business programs:** too expensive.
- **Athabasca:** online only.
- **MacEwan and Mount Royal:** no master's programs.

## Q4. PGWP check for the survivor (University of Regina M.Eng.)

### Takeaway
The University of Regina is a public DLI, number O19425660270 per canada.ca. The M.Eng. (30 credit hours, about 5 terms, on campus) appears to meet the 8-month and 50% in-class rules. The DLI number came from an IRCC page about a different program, so whether Regina appears on the **PGWP-eligible tab** of the DLI list is UNCONFIRMED. Master's graduates have no field-of-study requirement, but they need CLB 7 in all four abilities from an approved test. IELTS Academic will not satisfy this.

### Cited Findings
- The program must have been "at least 8 months long" at a PGWP-eligible DLI. For lock-in dates on or after 1 Sep 2024, at least 50% of the program must be completed "in class within Canada" (paraphrase) — [Canada.ca PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- Master's graduates have no field-of-study requirement. Applicants need CLB 7 / NCLC 7 in all 4 abilities, and test results must be less than 2 years old — [Canada.ca PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- Express Entry CLB 7 equivalents (not a PGWP-specific page):
  - IELTS General Training: 6.0 in each ability
  - CELPIP: 7 in each ability
  - PTE Core: score ranges per ability
  - [Canada.ca Express Entry: Language test results](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html)
- "DLI name: University of Regina. DLI number: O19425660270" appears on IRCC's Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot page, which is not a PGWP page — [Canada.ca FMC pilot participating DLIs](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/fmc-student-pilot/eligibility/participating-dlis.html)
- The DLI list has a PGWP-eligible tab, and "Not all programs offered by a DLI are eligible" (paraphrase) — [Canada.ca DLI list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html)

### Inferences
- A 30-credit-hour M.Eng. at 6 credit hours per term runs about 5 terms (about 20 months), well over 8 months. At 9 credit hours per term it is about 4 terms. The PGWP length would likely be up to 3 years, because master's programs of at least 8 months generally qualify for 3 years. This was not verified on canada.ca in this session.

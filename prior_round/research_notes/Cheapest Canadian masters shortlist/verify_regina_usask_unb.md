## D. PGWP (canada.ca)

### Takeaway
The canada.ca rules are re-confirmed (search excerpts):
- The program must be at least 8 months long.
- Master's graduates can get a 3-year PGWP even if the program was under 2 years.
- Master's graduates have no field-of-study requirement.
- CLB 7 is required in all 4 abilities.
- For lock-in dates on or after 1 Sep 2024, at least 50% of the program must be completed in class within Canada.

Regina's DLI number (O19425660270) is on a canada.ca page, but not the PGWP tab. No DLI number or PGWP-eligible flag was surfaced for USask or UNB. **The PGWP-eligible flag is UNCONFIRMED for all three.** All three are public universities, and degree programs at public universities are normally PGWP-eligible, but that is an inference.

### Cited Findings
**Program rules** — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- "completed a program of study at a PGWP-eligible designated learning institution that was at least 8 months long" (search excerpt)
- "apply for a 3-year PGWP, even if your master's program was less than 2 years" (search excerpt)
- For lock-in dates on or after 1 Sep 2024: "at least 50% of your program in class within Canada" (search excerpt)

**Field of study and language**
- "bachelor's degree, master's degree or doctoral degree" graduates do not need to meet the field-of-study requirement (search excerpt) — [PGWP: Field of study](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study.html)

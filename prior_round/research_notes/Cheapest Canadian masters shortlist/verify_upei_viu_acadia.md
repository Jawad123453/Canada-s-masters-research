## A. UPEI Master of Arts in Island Studies (MAIS), course-based option

### Takeaway
MAIS is still the strongest Atlantic candidate. It states a percentage bar of "B, i.e. 70% to 79.9%" over the last two years, and it publishes a **program-specific per-course international fee ($972)**. On that basis the 2026-27 cost is about **CAD 28.1k**, which is under 30k but above the 27k fee-increase line. Fall and Winter intakes are stated.

Two items still decide the outcome:
- **Grade averaging.** No rule was found on how UPEI averages repeated or failed courses for applicants. The applicant is about 73% if retakes replace failures, and about 66-67% if all attempts count.
- **Fee basis.** UPEI also publishes a general "$9,718 per year" full-time graduate international fee. Its interaction with the MAIS per-course line is not confirmed in writing.

### Cited Findings
**Bar (F1)**
- "ordinarily be expected to have achieved an average of at least second class ("B", i.e. 70% to 79.9%) standing in the work of the last four semesters or the last two undergraduate years" (search excerpt). This is the Faculty of Arts minimum quoted on the MAIS page. Degree basis: "Bachelor's degree, or equivalent, in an honours or majors program" (search excerpt). — [UPEI MAIS program page](https://www.upei.ca/programs/island-studies); [UPEI Calendar MAIS](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/master-of-arts-ma-island-studies/)
- Repeated/failed courses in the **admission** average: no UPEI graduate rule found. Graduate regulations cover only in-program fails: "<60% ... deemed to have failed". MA candidates "must maintain a cumulative average grade of at least 75% in the substantive courses" (search excerpt). That 75% is an **in-program progression risk**, not an admission rule. — [UPEI Graduate Program Regulations](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/program-regulations-graduate-studies/)
- UPEI's undergraduate-oriented SAP policy: all attempts recorded, "highest grade calculated into the GPA" (search excerpt). It is not a graduate admission rule and should not be relied on. — [UPEI SAP policy PDF](https://files.upei.ca/studentaffairs/upei_satisfactory_academic_progress_sap_policy.pdf)
- WES:
  - No UPEI-wide graduate WES requirement was found. A WES course-by-course evaluation is required only on the DVM page. — [UPEI Calendar DVM](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/doctor-of-veterinary-medicine/)
  - The graduate admissions chapter asks applicants from outside North America for official statements of grades and subject matter. Other programs (MEd) ask for a notarized translation (paraphrase). — [UPEI Graduate Program Admissions](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/graduate-program-admissions/)
  - UPEI "will not pre-evaluate" transcripts (paraphrase). — [UPEI Graduate Admissions](https://www.upei.ca/admission-requirements/graduate-admissions)
- Lebanon equivalency for graduate admission: none found in this round. The country page entries found earlier were undergraduate. — [UPEI country-specific requirements](https://www.upei.ca/admission-requirements/country-specific-requirements)

## B. Vancouver Island University: MEd in Educational Leadership (MEDL), full-time face-to-face option, Nanaimo

### Takeaway
MEDL is cheap: about CAD 25.1-25.6k mandatory on 2026-27 rates, or 26.0-26.5k with textbooks.
- **Cost correction:** an earlier note double-counted the $570 VIUSU health and dental (see arithmetic below).
- **Delivery:** face-to-face in Nanaimo, with a course-based Year 2 option.
- **Bar:** a "B" in the final two years, which at VIU means **72-75%**. The applicant clears it only if retakes replace failures (≈73%).
- **Prerequisites:** the degree is "preferably" a B.Ed and must be "acceptable in content to the Faculty of Education". Teaching experience is "two years preferred". That is a soft barrier for a BME graduate.
- **Remaining unknowns:** IELTS thresholds conflict across VIU documents, and no VIU page states that this program is PGWP-eligible.

### Cited Findings
- **Admission wording:** "Completion of a Bachelor's degree, (preferably a Bachelor of Education degree, or equivalent), from a recognized university that is acceptable in content to the Faculty of Education with either: a cumulative grade point average of "B" in the final two years and/or demonstration of academic excellence" (search excerpt). No percentage is stated. — [VIU MEDL program page](https://www.viu.ca/programs/education/master-education-educational-leadership)
- **Experience:** "A résumé outlining teaching experience (two years preferred)" (search excerpt). Other documents: 3 references, a statement of career interest, and a letter of intent. — [VIU MEDL](https://www.viu.ca/programs/education/master-education-educational-leadership); [MEDL Montessori option](https://education.viu.ca/medl-montessori-educator-option)

## C. Acadia University: any course-based route open to a BME graduate?

### Takeaway
**No.**
- The only course-based (non-thesis) master's found are the **MEd** streams. They require "two years of full-time, paid, relevant experience" and a "B average (73-76%)", so they fail F1 and F6.
- The MSc Computer Science course route needs a 4-year CS degree, so it fails F6 (earlier notes).
- The MA and MSc programs are thesis-based, so they fail F4.
- Acadia's general bar (B-/70%) and cost (27.6k) would otherwise qualify.

### Cited Findings
- **General bar:** "2.67/4.0 GPA OR a 70% in the courses taken in the major field in the last two undergraduate years" (search excerpt). The 2026-27 calendar sets B- (70%) over the last two years (or 60h). — [Acadia Graduate Admissions](https://www2.acadiau.ca/admissions/graduate-application.html); [Acadia Academic Calendar 2026-27](https://registrar.acadiau.ca/files/sites/registrar/pdfs/Academic_Calendars/Academic%20Calendar%202026-2027.pdf)

  - Two-year program line: tuition $17,726.08, total $19,906.82 (unclear basis).
  - Per course: $3,286.14.
  - "fees typically increase by 3-5% per year" (paraphrase).
  - Source: [Acadia full-time fees](https://www2.acadiau.ca/student-services/student-accounts/tuition-fees/full-time-student-fees.html)
- **MEd route:**
  - Curriculum Studies (General) is 30 credits, with 3 required courses and 7 electives. Non-thesis students take "21h electives" (search excerpt).
  - Admission: "you must have accumulated the equivalent of two years of full-time, paid, relevant experience" and "B average (73-76%) in their final two years" (search excerpt).

## D. PGWP (canada.ca) and DLI status

### Takeaway
canada.ca rules are favourable to all three schools' master's in principle:
- no field-of-study requirement for master's;
- a 3-year PGWP if the program is at least 8 months;
- at least 50% in class in Canada;
- full-time every term except the last;
- CLB 7 in all four skills.

The live DLI table's "PGWP-eligible" flag could not be rendered for any of the three, so it is **UNCONFIRMED**.

### Cited Findings
- **Field of study:** "no field of study requirements for graduates with a bachelor's degree, master's degree or doctoral degree" (search excerpt). — [PGWP field of study](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study.html)
- **Length:** master's graduates can get "a 3-year PGWP, even if your master's program was less than 2 years", provided it is at least 8 months (search excerpt). — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- **Distance learning:** for lock-in on or after Sept 1, 2024, "complete at least 50% of your program in class within Canada" (search excerpt). — same page
- **Full-time status:** "maintained full-time status as a student in Canada during each semester ... (except your final semester ...)" (search excerpt). — same page
- **Language:** CLB 7 in all 4 areas for university graduates (applications from Nov 1, 2024). — same page; [PGWP language results](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/language-results.html)
- **DLI list:** "includes all DLIs that currently offer programs of study that make you eligible for the PGWP"; "Not all programs offered by a DLI are eligible" (search excerpt). The UPEI, VIU and Acadia rows were not rendered. — [IRCC DLI list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html)

## Q1. Which BC and Territories master's programs are cheapest, and which clear the under-CAD-30,000 cost filter?

### Takeaway
On sticker price, UNBC is the cheapest in BC, followed by UVic and SFU research-stream fees. All three fail the grade bar (Filter 1). Among programs that are actually reachable, only **VIU's Master of Education in Educational Leadership (MEDL)** clears cost, at about CAD 25.7k–26.5k all-in on 2026-27 rates. TRU, Royal Roads, Emily Carr, VIU's other master's and UBC Vancouver/Okanagan all fail cost or sit at its edge. Yukon University, Aurora College, Capilano and KPU have no admitting own master's degree.

## Q4. PGWP check for the survivor (VIU MEDL)

### Takeaway
The program design appears to meet PGWP rules: a degree of 8 months or longer, face-to-face in Canada, and a master's that is exempt from the field-of-study requirement. Two items are UNCONFIRMED: VIU's presence on the DLI list with the PGWP flag, and the page date. Because VIU appears to accept only IELTS Academic, the applicant would need a separate CLB 7 test (IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core) for the PGWP.

### Cited Findings
- The program must be at least 8 months long. For lock-in dates from Sept 1, 2024: "You must complete at least 50% of your program in class within Canada" (verbatim excerpt). [canada.ca PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- Since Nov 1, 2024, university graduates need **CLB 7**. Master's graduates remain eligible "regardless of their field of study" (verbatim fragment), provided they meet the language requirement. [canada.ca PGWP eligibility](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)

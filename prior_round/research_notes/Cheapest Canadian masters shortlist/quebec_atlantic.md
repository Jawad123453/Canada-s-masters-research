## Q1. Atlantic Canada — which programs survive the six filters?

### Takeaway
Only ONE Atlantic program cleared all six filters on official text: **UPEI Master of Arts in Island Studies — course-based option** (≈ CAD 28.1k on 2026-27 rates; bar "B, i.e., 70% to 79.9%" over the last two years). It is a PROVISIONAL PASS with important gaps (repeated-course averaging, 2027-28 fee increase pushing toward ~29.9k, full-time/in-class confirmation, 2027 intake). Every other Atlantic program screened fails, mostly on F1 (plain B/3.0 bars at UNB, Dal, MSVU, Moncton, SMU) or F3 (CBU, SMU business programs, UNB MBA).

## Q2. Quebec — can any course-based master's come in under CAD 30k for a Lebanese applicant?

### Takeaway
No. Since Fall 2024 the Quebec government fixes a minimum international supplement for non-research master's: base CAD 103.92/credit + MES forfait CAD 639.78/credit = **CAD 743.70/credit floor (2026-27)**. A standard 45-credit master's therefore costs at least 45 × 743.70 = **CAD 33,466.50 in tuition alone** → every Quebec course-based master's FAILS F3. The regional exemption ends for new students from Fall 2026; Lebanon has a small country-agreement quota (~20 master's-level exemptions, 2023 data) but it is nominated by Lebanese authorities and its 2027 status is UNCONFIRMED — not budgetable.

## Q3. Best survivor — UPEI Master of Arts in Island Studies (course-based option): full verification

### Takeaway
PROVISIONAL PASS on all six filters. Estimated total ≈ **CAD 28,132 (2026-27 rates)** → fee-increase risk; with one more year of UPEI's 2026-27 increases (+6.5% tuition, +7.5% international) ≈ **CAD 29,862**, i.e. razor-thin under 30k. Decisive open item: how UPEI computes the last-two-years average (≈73% if retakes replace failures → PASS; ≈66-67% if all attempts count → below the "70%" bar).

### Inferences
- UPEI is the cheapest compliant option found in Quebec + Atlantic, but the margin under 30k is small and depends on 2027-28 fees, on finishing in ~2 years, and on the per-course international fee basis.
- For PR purposes, an Island Studies MA is a non-technical field; PGWP has no field-of-study restriction for master's degrees (Q4), so this does not block PGWP.

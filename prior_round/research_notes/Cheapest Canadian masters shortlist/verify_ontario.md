  - 6 semesters (if "24 months" is billed at fees-per-semester only): 24,141 + 6 × 937.21 (5,623.26) + 2 × 948 = **31,660.26**, which FAILS.
  - **Verdict:** PASS at 3–4 semesters, with fee-increase risk (≥27k). Compulsory fees for 2027-28 and 2028-29 will rise, so 4 semesters could cross 30k.
  - The $2,000 tuition deposit for international course-based students is credited toward tuition, so it is not an extra cost.
- **Filter 1 is the likely failure point:**

  2. If Guelph converts through a US-style GPA, its own mapping of 70% to 3.0 means WES 2.68 (and the ~2.90 last-two-years figure) fails.
  3. The pass case needs the in-house assessment to treat a ~73% last-two-years Lebanese average (retakes replacing failures) as ≥70%. The available evidence makes that unlikely.

- **Honours:** "or the equivalent". The current guideline lists a 4-year Lebanese Licence as the master's minimum (paraphrase), so a 4-year (here 5-year) BME likely counts. Not explicitly confirmed.
- **Filters 4, 5 and 6:** PASS. No supervisor (the Graduate Program Coordinator advises); on campus; any discipline accepted.
- **PGWP length:** three or more semesters is more than 8 months, so it PASSES.

## E. PGWP checks (canada.ca)

### Takeaway
- **DLI status:** PGWP-eligible status for Guelph, Lakehead and Algoma could still not be confirmed. The DLI list is a dynamic search table that the search tool does not index.
- **Rules:** canada.ca restates the core rules: a PGWP-eligible DLI, at least 8 months, full-time, and at least 50% in class in Canada for lock-in dates on or after 1 Sept 2024.
- **Campuses:** DLI numbers and PGWP eligibility are per campus and per program. For Algoma, check whether the Sault Ste. Marie or Brampton campus is listed on the letter of acceptance.

### Cited Findings
- Eligibility: "you completed a program of study at a PGWP-eligible designated learning institution that was at least 8 months long" and "you apply for your PGWP within 180 days" (search excerpts). — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- Distance learning: "if less than 50% of the program of study's total courses are completed by distance learning, a post-graduation work permit may be issued" (search excerpt). Programs taken exclusively by distance learning are ineligible (paraphrase). — [IRCC PGWP eligibility requirements (program delivery instructions)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/study-permits/post-graduation-work-permit-program/eligibility.html)
- For lock-in dates on or after 1 Sept 2024, at least 50% must be completed in class within Canada, and time studied outside Canada is deducted (paraphrase). — [Transition period for distance learning measures](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/distance-learning-measures.html)
- Language results are required for most applicants from 1 Nov 2024 (paraphrase). CLB 7 is required in all four abilities, with IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core accepted (earlier round). — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- PGWP eligibility is set per program and per campus, with each campus having its own DLI number. Being at a DLI is "not enough" (paraphrase). — [Designated learning institutions list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html)
- "Date modified" stamps were not visible in any excerpt.

### Inferences

- For Algoma, the 16-month MCS specialization runs in Brampton. The 12-month MCS campus is unknown. No IRCC caveat specific to Algoma's Brampton campus was found.

Research date: 23 Oct 2026. Evidence comes only from the universities' own websites, plus canada.ca for PGWP rules. Aggregators were not used as evidence.

> **How the evidence was collected (the report writer must keep this caveat):**
> - WebFetch failed with DNS errors ("getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND") on every host tried (lakeheadu.ca, uoguelph.ca, canada.ca).

> - As a result, no official page could be opened in full. All evidence below comes from WebSearch runs restricted (allowed_domains) to the university's own domain, or to canada.ca.
> - Text in "quotation marks" is the verbatim excerpt the search tool returned from that official URL. Text marked "paraphrase" is the search tool's summary of the official page, not a verbatim quote.

## Q1. Re-verify Lakehead University, Master of Environmental Management (MEM), 12-month course-based

### Takeaway
- **Cost:** The MEM is cheap, at about CAD 23.4k–25.0k including UHIP. It is course-based with no supervisor, and is taught at the Thunder Bay campus.
- **Bar:** Lakehead's bar is "second class (B)", and Lakehead's B means 70–79%.
- **Likely failure point:** Lakehead's own Lebanon grade table appears to place 12–14/20 in the C band and 15–17/20 in the B band. The applicant's 14.1/20 would then fall below the bar.
- **Verdict:** UNCONFIRMED, leaning FAIL on Filter 1.

### Cited Findings
**Admission bar (Filter 1)**
- The general master's standard is a four-year bachelor's with at least a B (second class) across the last 20 half courses (paraphrase). This is a last-two-years basis. — [Lakehead Masters admission requirements](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/admissions/graduate-admissions-requirements/masters-programs/node/52385)
- Program and international wording: international applicants are "recommended to have an overall standing of Second Class - Upper Division or higher". For British-patterned systems: "A minimum grade of B, or Second Class, Upper Division, is required." — [MEM program page](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/environmental-management); [Lakehead graduate engineering admission (same rule)](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/engineering-civil/node/)
- Numeric meaning of B at Lakehead, from the graduate grading scale: "A 80 to 89% B 70 to 79% C 60 to 69%". So "second class (B)" means at least 70%. — [Lakehead graduate Grading System](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/departments/womensstudies/faculty-instructor-resources/information-and-regulations-for-graduate-students/grading-system)

  - Search excerpts show: 18–20 → "A-, A, A+"; 15–17 → "B-, B, B+"; 12–14 → "C-, C, C+"; 10–11 → "D-, D, D+".

  - The degree row lists Lebanon "Bachelor's, Licence requiring 4 years of study" as equivalent to a Canadian four-year bachelor's.
  - Source: [Lakehead International Credential Equivalency by Country](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/academic-information/international-credential-equivalency)
- Qualifying year: "A Qualifying Year at the undergraduate level may be required" (paraphrase-level excerpt). — [Lakehead Masters admission requirements](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/admissions/graduate-admissions-requirements/masters-programs/node/52385)

### Inferences
- **Cost arithmetic.** Both estimates assume 3 billed terms. The MEM-specific fee line was not found.
  - Generic table: 3 × 7,468.98 = 22,406.94; plus UHIP 948 = **CAD 23,354.94**.
  - 2026-27 per-term rate: 3 × 8,026.58 = 24,079.74; plus 948 = **CAD 25,027.74**.
  - Both are under 30k and under 27k, so no fee-increase flag. Fall 2027 rates are not yet published.
- **Lebanon row.**
  - If the "B-/B/B+" letters are the Lakehead equivalents (the most natural reading of a "Lakehead Grade Equivalent" column), the applicant's 14.1/20 (and every year average up to 14.9) maps to C+. That fails the "B (second class)" bar, because the B band starts at 15/20.
  - If instead 12–14 maps to "70-79%", the applicant passes.
  - Because the B band likely starts at 15/20, this is UNCONFIRMED and leaning FAIL. The same Lebanon table would apply to every Lakehead master's, so no other Lakehead program was screened.
- **Average basis.** The rule uses the last 20 half courses, which helps the applicant (≈2.90/4.0 for 2024-26). On the /20 scale, however, the last two years would still need ≥15/20.
- **IELTS.** 7.0 passes the overall bar, provided every sub-band is ≥6.0. Only Academic is accepted, so for the PGWP the applicant will later need IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core at CLB 7.

### Gaps

- Whether Lakehead accepts a WES evaluation or evaluates the transcript itself: not found. The country table suggests an in-house evaluation.

## Q2. Re-verify University of Guelph, Capacity Development & Extension (course-based option / "Master of Capacity Development and Extension")

### Takeaway
- **Bar:** Guelph's general bar is explicitly "70% (B-) average ... in the last two years". The course-based CDE page says "second class honours (B-)".
- **Cost:** The 2027-28 program tuition is approved at $24,141 for three semesters. With compulsory fees and UHIP, three semesters total about **CAD 27,900**, which is under 30k but carries fee-increase risk. If the program runs a 4th semester (the calendar says "three to four semesters"), the total goes above 30k.
- **Other problems:** A Guelph departmental PDF (age unknown) lists the Lebanon minimum as "80% or 15/20". There is also an "honours" requirement. Intake is Fall only.
- **Verdict:** UNCONFIRMED (a conditional PASS only if 3 semesters are billed and the current Lebanon row is ≤70%/14/20).

- **Lebanon row:** If the old "15/20" row is still in force, the applicant (14.1/20) fails Filter 1.
- **Honours requirement:** Guelph requires an "honours program or the equivalent". The current guideline lists a 4-year Lebanese Licence as the minimum, so this is probably satisfied. It is still a flag, because WES did not describe the degree as honours.
- **Supervisor (Filter 4):** No supervisor has to be found. The Graduate Program Coordinator acts as advisor, so this PASSES.
- **Degree background (Filter 6):** PASSES. Experience is preferred, not required.

### Gaps

- Whether full-time course-based students can finish in 3 billed semesters.
- 2027-28 compulsory fees and UHIP.
- IELTS sub-band minimums, and whether General Training is accepted. Treat it as Academic only.
- Guelph's rule on repeated or failed courses, and whether it accepts WES: not found.
- Whether "Master of Capacity Development and Extension" is now a distinct degree name from the "MSc CDE course-based option". The pages use both names.

## Q5. PGWP check (canada.ca)

### Takeaway
- **Field of study:** Master's graduates are exempt from the field-of-study requirement.
- **Language:** CLB 7 in all four skills is required, using IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core. The applicant's existing IELTS (type unknown) only counts if it is General Training.
- **Program rules:** The program must be ≥8 months, full-time and in Canada, with less than 50% by distance learning.
- **DLI status:** Each candidate's public-DLI / PGWP-eligible status could not be confirmed, because the DLI list is a dynamic table that the search tool cannot read.

### Cited Findings
- Graduates from university bachelor's, master's and doctoral programs "will remain eligible for a PGWP, regardless of their field of study, provided that they meet the minimum language requirement" (paraphrase from the canada.ca update). — [Update on field of study requirement for PGWPs](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/update-requirement-post-graduation-work-permits.html); [PGWP field of study requirement](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study.html)
- The program must be "a minimum of 8 months in duration" and in Canada, with full-time enrolment. — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
- Distance learning: "if less than 50% of the program of study's total courses are completed by distance learning, a PGWP may be issued" (operational guidance). — [IRCC PGWP Program instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/study-permits/post-graduation-work-permit-program.html)
- Language requirement:
  - Minimum "CLB 7" in English (or NCLC 7) "in all 4 language areas". — [PGWP: Who can apply](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html)
  - Accepted English tests are "CELPIP-General", "IELTS General Training" and "PTE Core"; results must be less than 2 years old. — [PGWP: Get the right documents](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/get-documents.html); [PGWP language results](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/language-results.html)
- The DLI list has a PGWP-eligible tab, and "not all programs offered by a DLI are eligible for a PGWP" (paraphrase). — [Designated learning institutions list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html)

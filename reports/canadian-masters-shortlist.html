Application checklist (Regina and UNB)

  1. Online application and the $125 fee (one per program).
  2. WES ICAP course-by-course report sent by WES directly to the University of Regina (mandatory for Engineering; optional at UNB).
  3. Transcript and degree certificate.
  4. CV, and a letter of intent (Regina's 4-question template; Regina states that using generative AI to write it counts as plagiarism) or a 1–2 page statement of purpose (UNB).
  5. References: at least 2 for Regina, submitted by referees from institutional email addresses; 3 for UNB, at least 2 academic.
  6. The English test score, sent directly by the testing organization.

Money

  • IRCC proof of funds: first-year tuition + $23,448 living (single applicant, outside Quebec, for applications from 1 September 2026) + travel. Accepted proof includes 6 months of bank statements, a GIC, a tuition receipt or a sponsor's letter with their documents. Funds from future work in Canada do not count.
  • Regina payment: tuition is "due on the first day of classes". A payment plan (3% fee) splits a fall term into four instalments: at sign-up, 1 October, 2 November and 1 December. Unpaid balances over $2,000 block registration changes.
  • Rent (CMHC, October 2025 average): Regina 1-bedroom $1,238, 2-bedroom $1,473; Fredericton $1,203 / $1,413; Brandon $810 / $1,086.
  • Residences: Regina College West shared apartment $3,202 per term (utilities and internet included); UNB graduate suite room $8,485 for 8 months; Brandon double $2,300 per term plus a required meal plan from $2,425.
  • Realistic 12-month living budget, shared apartment: about $17,700 in Regina, $17,400 in Fredericton, $18,100 in Brandon.

Ways to lower the cost

  • Finish in the fewest terms the program allows.
  • Work part-time: up to 24 hours a week off campus during terms; no limit on campus.
  • Graduate teaching assistantships at Regina pay about $2,909 per term for master's students, but only "fully-qualified" students are eligible and positions are competitive.
  • Regina U-Pass opt-out ($90 per term) if living in residence or within 1 km of campus; UNB emergency insurance opt-out ($200 per term) once on NB Medicare.
  • Federal tuition tax credit (15%) once working and filing Canadian tax returns every year.
  • Not available: Regina entrance scholarships (80%+, thesis or Regina degree), Regina's tuition reimbursement (undergraduates only), Saskatchewan Graduate Retention Program ("Master's and PhD degrees are not eligible"), New Brunswick and Manitoba tuition rebates (cancelled).

After graduation

  • PGWP: a master's of at least 8 months gives "a 3-year PGWP", with CLB 7 from an in-person IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core test (Duolingo and IELTS Academic are not accepted).
  • Canadian Experience Class: 1 year (1,560 hours) of skilled paid work in Canada after studies; work done while studying full-time does not count. A Canadian credential of one or two years earns 15 CRS points either way, so a shorter program saves only a few months.

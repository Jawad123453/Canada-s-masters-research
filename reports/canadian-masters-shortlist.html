Assumptions and limits

  • Evidence. This round opened every official page in full, which last round could not do. Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. Anything else is marked "(paraphrase)" or UNCONFIRMED. Pages read only through a third party are labelled as such.
  • IELTS version. Your 7.0 overall and 7.0 in each band clears every finalist's requirement. Every finalist names only IELTS Academic, and none mentions General Training. The report assumes your result is Academic. If it is General Training, Filter 2 becomes UNCONFIRMED at all five, and you may need to sit the Academic test.
  • PGWP language test. For the PGWP itself, IRCC accepts only CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training, PTE Core, TEF Canada or TCF Canada, taken in person and less than 2 years old. University graduates need CLB 7 in all four skills, which for IELTS General Training means 6.0 in each band. IELTS Academic does not count, so plan a General Training (or CELPIP/PTE Core) test near graduation.
  • Fee year. Fall 2027 entrants pay 2027-28 rates, which no finalist has published. Totals use 2026-27 rates, followed by a projection that uses each school's own stated increase:
  • Regina: +3.0% for 2026-27.
  • VIU: "expected to increase by 2% each year".
  • UPEI: +6.5% tuition and +7.5% international fee for 2026-27.

Any total of $27,000 or more is flagged fee-increase risk. - Total cost means international tuition plus mandatory fees plus compulsory health insurance for the whole program. It excludes living costs, textbooks and deposits that are credited to tuition. - "Last two years" of your 5-year degree is assumed to mean years 4 and 5 (2024-25 and 2025-26). No school confirmed this. - Gap counting. "Preferred" is not counted as a requirement under Filter 6. A total that is under $30,000 at published rates passes Filter 3, with the risk flag where it applies. - Already eliminated programs from your list were not re-researched.
